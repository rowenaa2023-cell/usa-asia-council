Feminist Foreign Policy in Theory and in Practice

Date & Time August 14, 2024
1:00 PM – 4:30 PM EST
Location / Venue Carnegie Endowment for International Peace & Global Webcast, Washington, DC
Featured Speakers Amb. Melanne Verveer, International Human Rights Lawyers, UN Women Envoys

Executive Overview

This landmark conference convened leading diplomats, researchers, and civil society advocates from across North America, Europe, and Asia to examine the transformative impact of Feminist Foreign Policy frameworks in conflict prevention, climate negotiations, and international development.

Key Deliberations & Strategic Briefing

Panelists showcased empirical evidence demonstrating that peace agreements where women participate as meaningful signatories are 35% more likely to endure for at least 15 years. Discussions focused on translating high-level gender equality commitments into operational defense and foreign assistance budget allocations.

Case studies evaluated grassroots peacebuilding initiatives in Southeast Asia and community-led climate adaptation projects in South Asia, underscoring how inclusive policy design enhances global stability.

Strategic Policy Takeaways

  • Release of the Comparative Feminist Foreign Policy Scorecard across G7 and Asian partner nations.
  • Actionable metrics for measuring gender-responsive budgeting in bilateral aid programs.
  • Establishment of the Women, Peace, and Security Diplomatic Working Network.
  • Policy recommendations for integrating human rights benchmarks into formal trade pacts.
"Sustainable peace is impossible when half the population is excluded from the negotiating table. Inclusion is not an afterthought; it is the cornerstone of lasting security." — Amb. Melanne Verveer
