CONGRESSIONAL FORUM

INDO-PACIFIC DIPLOMATIC FORUM WITH CONGRESSMAN ANDY KIM

Date & Time July 9, 2026
2:00 PM – 4:30 PM EST
Location / Venue Rayburn House Office Building & Congressional Corridor, Washington, DC
Featured Participants Representative Andy Kim, Council Executive Director Faraz Ullah, Congressional National Security Staff

Executive Overview

Council Executive Director Faraz Ullah met with Representative Andy Kim in Washington, DC, for a comprehensive dialogue on Indo-Pacific diplomacy, East Asian security architecture, and fostering bipartisan support for international diplomatic engagement.

Key Deliberations & Strategic Outcomes

The consultation addressed deterrence and crisis de-escalation protocols in the Taiwan Strait and East China Sea, strengthening civilian technology alliances between the United States and democratic Asian allies, and supporting diplomatic families and service members stationed across the Pacific theater.

Strategic Policy Takeaways

  • Bipartisan strategies for reinforcing diplomatic deterrence while maintaining open channels of communication.
  • Expansion of congressional caucuses championing peaceful Indo-Pacific trade and security alliances.
  • Analysis of economic resilience strategies to mitigate semiconductor supply shocks.
  • Enhanced support for Asian-American foreign service officers and policy analysts.
"Smart, principled foreign policy requires constant engagement with both allies and regional partners to prevent miscalculation and preserve peace." — USA-ASIA Council Diplomatic Assembly
