The USA-Asia Council Policy Briefs Series serves as our flagship publication channel, delivering authoritative analysis and concrete legislative and diplomatic recommendations to senior policymakers across Washington and partner capitals in Asia.

Strategic Recommendations for the 119th Congress & Executive Leadership

  • Implement Transpacific Tech Standards: Harmonize cybersecurity, data sovereignty, and cloud storage compliance between the U.S. and ASEAN digital economies.
  • Scale Green Transition Financing: Deploy U.S. International Development Finance Corporation (DFC) equity into high-impact solar, hydro, and grid modernization projects in developing Asian nations.
  • Bilateral Trade Facilitation Accords: Digitize customs clearing and tariff harmonization to accelerate agricultural and high-tech trade through Pacific ports.
