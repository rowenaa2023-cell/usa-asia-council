OUR MISSION & VISION

Strengthening Principled American Global Leadership & Strategic Alliances

USA-Asia Council Foreign Policy Partnership engages, informs, and mobilizes leaders, scholars, and policymakers committed to advancing principled American engagement, economic prosperity, and strategic stability across Asia.

Foreign policy begins at home. We believe that America’s security and prosperity depend on robust diplomacy, strong alliances, active multilateralism, and defending democratic values across the globe—with special emphasis on strengthening USA–Asia cooperation for a stable global future.

Who We Are

USA-Asia Council Foreign Policy Partnership is a premier non-partisan policy organization dedicated to advancing constructive transpacific diplomatic and economic relations. We work with congressional leadership, executive agencies, allied governments, and civil society to champion policies that safeguard regional stability, secure semiconductor supply chains, expand clean energy infrastructure, and foster deep cultural understanding.

Through our research working groups, congressional briefings for the 119th Congress, and Track 1.5 diplomacy, we connect American innovators, diplomats, military veterans, and scholars with Asian partners to build an inclusive foreign policy that advances shared prosperity.

Our Core Pillars

Diplomacy First

Prioritizing peaceful negotiations, international treaties, and revitalization of the State Department over unilateral military action.

Strengthening Alliances

Deepening NATO, Indo-Pacific partnerships, and bilateral cooperation with historic partners like Pakistan to promote collective security.

Democracy & Human Rights

Championing civic freedoms, anti-corruption safeguards, and international humanitarian law across the globe.

Global Health & Climate

Mobilizing clean technology investments and pandemic prevention networks to safeguard future generations.
