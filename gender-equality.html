Our Missions · Women, Peace & Security

Advancing Gender Equality Worldwide

Empirical data unequivocally demonstrates that nations where women participate fully in political, economic, and judicial institutions are vastly more peaceful, economically productive, and resilient against conflict and violent extremism. The status of women and girls is a direct barometer of global security.

Under the Women, Peace, and Security (WPS) Act, American diplomacy must mandate the inclusion of women negotiators in peace talks, ceasefire verifications, and post-conflict governance reconstructions. When women participate in peace negotiations, agreements are 35 percent more likely to endure for at least fifteen years.

We advocate for sustained global investments in girls' secondary education, combating gender-based violence in conflict zones, and eliminating legal barriers that prevent women entrepreneurs from owning land, accessing credit, and participating in global trade.
