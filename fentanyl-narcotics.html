Disrupting Fentanyl & Synthetic Narcotics Supply Chains

The illicit synthetic opioid crisis is an unprecedented public health and national security disaster claiming tens of thousands of American lives annually. Unlike traditional plant-based narcotics, fentanyl is manufactured in clandestine chemical laboratories using dual-use precursors sourced predominantly from chemical manufacturers in Asia, which are then trafficked by transnational cartels across our hemisphere.

Defeating this threat requires a sophisticated, whole-of-government foreign policy approach that attacks every node of the illicit supply chain. The United States must leverage intense diplomatic pressure, multilateral chemical scheduling under the UN Commission on Narcotic Drugs (CND), and collaborative intelligence sharing to compel source countries to enforce strict "know-your-customer" regulations on chemical exporters and illicit financial transactions.

Simultaneously, we advocate for deepening law enforcement partnerships with Mexico and Central American allies, targeting dark web synthetic marketplaces, and investing in advanced non-intrusive border screening technology to intercept shipments before they devastate local American communities.
