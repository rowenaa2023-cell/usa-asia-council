Where Foreign Policy Meets Domestic Prosperity (The Intermestic Link)

The USA-Asia Council Intermestic Policy Initiative investigates how international diplomatic decisions, trade corridors, and supply chain pacts in Asia directly power manufacturing, agriculture, and high-wage employment across the American homeland.

Transpacific commercial maritime shipping: Supporting millions of manufacturing and agricultural jobs across the United States.

Foreign policy is no longer confined to foreign capitals. In an interconnected global economy, the boundary between international strategy and domestic economic welfare has effectively vanished. The term "Intermestic"—the intersection of international and domestic—reflects the reality that decisions negotiated in Tokyo, Seoul, Singapore, or Islamabad immediately ripple through auto plants in Michigan, semiconductor foundries in Arizona and Ohio, and soybean farms in Iowa.

The USA-Asia Council Intermestic Initiative provides state-by-state data mapping how bilateral U.S.–Asia commerce sustains high-wage American jobs. In 2025–2026, two-way U.S.–Asia trade exceeded $1.9 Trillion, sustaining over 3.8 million American jobs. Over 28% of all U.S. agricultural exports—from Pacific Northwest wheat to Gulf Coast sorghum—are consumed across Asian markets.

Furthermore, the reshoring and friend-shoring of advanced semiconductor packaging under the CHIPS and Science Act relies on reliable, secure Asian supply chains for critical raw materials, testing, and wafer inputs. By demonstrating the tangible domestic dividends of principled foreign policy, we help build durable bipartisan consensus for American leadership in Asia.

Strategic Recommendations for the 119th Congress & Executive Leadership

  • Targeted Export Promotion for Heartland Producers: Modernize trade finance guarantees through EXIM Bank for small-and-medium agricultural and machinery exporters to Southeast and South Asia.
  • Allied Clean Energy Manufacturing Pacts: Establish joint bilateral credits for battery storage, solar polysilicon refining, and electric vehicle drivetrain production.
  • Port & Maritime Infrastructure Modernization: Accelerate West Coast and Gulf port throughput automation to eliminate maritime bottlenecks across transpacific shipping routes.
