Israeli-Palestinian Peace & Human Security

Lasting security for the state of Israel and self-determination and dignity for the Palestinian people are inseparable goals. The recurring cycles of devastation, extremism, and civilian tragedy prove that managing the conflict without a political resolution is untenable and dangerous to global stability.

U.S. leadership must actively support a negotiated two-state solution that provides genuine security guarantees for Israel alongside a viable, independent, and demilitarized Palestinian state. American policy must insist on full adherence to international humanitarian law, ensure the flow of unhindered humanitarian aid, and support moderate, legitimate governance institutions.

Diplomats must work with regional Arab partners to rebuild Gaza, halt unilateral settlement expansion in the West Bank, and anchor regional integration in a credible political pathway toward self-determination and lasting peace.
