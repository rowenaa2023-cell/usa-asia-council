Our Missions · Pandemic Preparedness

Global Health Security

Pathogens do not recognize national borders or sovereign checkpoints. In an interconnected global economy, an infectious outbreak in any remote region poses a direct and immediate threat to American lives, supply chains, and economic security.

U.S. leadership in global health security requires proactive investment in disease surveillance networks, rapid vaccine manufacturing capacity, and strengthening the World Health Organization (WHO) and PEPFAR. By assisting partner nations in Asia and Africa with laboratory diagnostics and bio-containment training, the United States preempts pandemic threats before they escalate into global crises.

We call on Congress to maintain permanent biodefense financing, support equitable global access to life-saving medical countermeasures, and defend medical neutrality in conflict zones.
