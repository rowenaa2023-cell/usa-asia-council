Our Missions · Foreign Service

Revitalizing American Diplomacy

Diplomacy is America's primary tool of international statecraft and crisis prevention. Sustainable global security cannot be achieved through military preeminence alone; it demands persistent, principled diplomatic engagement that builds coalitions, defuses tensions, and unlocks collaborative economic agreements.

Strengthening the Department of State and the U.S. Foreign Service requires investing in a resilient workforce equipped for 21st-century diplomacy. By prioritizing cultural fluency, economic statecraft, and technological literacy, diplomats can counter misinformation and outmaneuver authoritarian rivals across strategic regions in Asia, Africa, and the Middle East.

We advocate for sustained federal appropriations for foreign affairs, accelerated ambassadorial confirmations, and modernizing embassies to uphold American leadership in every corner of the world.
