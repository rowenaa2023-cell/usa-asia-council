Our Missions · European Stability

Defending Ukraine & European Security

Russia’s illegal and unprovoked invasion of Ukraine is the gravest threat to peace and the rules-based international order since World War II. Allowing Vladimir Putin’s war of imperial conquest to succeed would establish a catastrophic global precedent—signaling to aggressors everywhere that national sovereignty and international borders can be rewritten by force.

American support for Ukraine is not charity; it is a vital strategic investment in transatlantic stability and global deterrence. By supplying Ukraine with advanced air defense, precision artillery, and intelligence sharing alongside European allies, the United States has substantially degraded the Russian military's conventional threat to NATO without deploying a single American soldier.

We call on Congress to maintain decisive, sustained security assistance, enforce crippling sanctions against Russia’s military-industrial supply chain, hold war criminals legally accountable, and support Ukraine’s post-war reconstruction and Euro-Atlantic integration.
