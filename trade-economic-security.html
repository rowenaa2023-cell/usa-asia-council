Our Missions · Economic Statecraft

Trade & Economic Security in the 21st Century

Economic security is national security. In a hyper-connected global economy vulnerable to geopolitical coercion and supply shocks, the United States must balance competitive open trade with aggressive protection of critical technologies, intellectual property, and essential manufacturing capacity.

Rather than retreating into broad, indiscriminate tariffs that harm American consumers and alienate democratic allies, smart economic statecraft relies on targeted "de-risking" and "friend-shoring." By partnering with trusted nations across Asia and Europe, the U.S. can build resilient, diversified supply chains in advanced semiconductors, rare earth minerals, green energy technologies, and pharmaceuticals.

We advocate for high-standard trade agreements with enforceable labor and environmental protections, screening outbound investments in dual-use technologies, and revitalizing the World Trade Organization to hold non-market economies accountable for state-subsidized dumping.
