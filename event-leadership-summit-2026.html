Upcoming Summit & Gala

2026 Leadership Summit

Date & Time November 12, 2026
9:00 AM – 6:30 PM EST
Location / Venue Ronald Reagan Building and International Trade Center, Washington, DC
Featured Speakers Sen. Tim Kaine, Amb. Katherine Tai, Gen. CQ Brown Jr., Council Chair Dr. Elena Vance

Executive Overview

The 2026 Leadership Summit marks our flagship annual convention convening senior members of the United States Senate, House Foreign Affairs Committee, Department of State, and ministerial delegations from key Asian partners including Japan, South Korea, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and ASEAN member states. As strategic competition intensifies across the Indo-Pacific and West Asia, this summit sets a clear, bipartisan roadmap for proactive diplomacy, regional deterrence, and durable trade alliances.

Key Deliberations & Strategic Briefing

The summit's morning sessions focus on revitalizing bilateral security architectures while bolstering economic resilience against global supply chain shocks. Delegates will examine how modern alliance networks can address both traditional maritime threats and non-traditional challenges such as cyber vulnerabilities, critical mineral access, and humanitarian logistics.

In the afternoon policy breakout tracks, participants will review high-level reports on regional conflict de-escalation, multilateral trade agreements, and energy security partnerships across Central and South Asia. The day will conclude with our Annual Diplomatic Gala and the presentation of the 2026 Global Statesmanship Awards.

Strategic Policy Takeaways

  • Establishment of the 2026 Bipartisan Asian Strategic Working Group in Congress.
  • Framework agreement on critical supply chain mutual assistance protocols across maritime Indo-Pacific lanes.
  • Targeted diplomatic initiatives to deepen trade relations with Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and ASEAN allies without regional escalation.
  • Expanded fellowship programs connecting emerging Asian policymakers with Capitol Hill staff.
"American engagement in Asia is not merely about deterrence; it is about building deep, durable institutional partnerships that foster reciprocal prosperity and peace." — USA-ASIA Council Summit Steering Committee
