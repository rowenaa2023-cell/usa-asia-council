Upcoming Strategic Roundtable

Pacific Maritime Security & Climate Resilience

Date & Time September 19, 2026
1:00 PM – 7:00 PM HST
Location / Venue East-West Center, Honolulu, Hawaii
Featured Speakers USINDOPACOM Commander, Pacific Island Forum Ambassadors, Coast Guard Vice Admirals

Executive Overview

Focusing on the intersection of maritime law enforcement, commercial shipping freedom, and accelerating climate vulnerabilities, this high-level forum unites island nation leaders, naval tacticians, and international legal scholars. Unchecked illegal fishing, sea-level rise, and aggressive maritime boundary assertions demand a cohesive, rules-based diplomatic response.

Key Deliberations & Strategic Briefing

Delegates will assess real-time domain awareness systems, satellite-linked maritime surveillance networks, and joint humanitarian assistance/disaster relief (HADR) exercises. The roundtable emphasizes non-escalatory, legal mechanisms rooted in the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS) to ensure navigational openness across the Malacca Strait, South China Sea, and South Pacific.

Furthermore, island resilience packages integrating American renewable microgrids and desalination infrastructure will be presented to bolster climate security for vulnerable coastal and atoll communities.

Strategic Policy Takeaways

  • Deployment of integrated automated tracking for IUU (illegal, unreported, unregulated) fishing vessels.
  • Tri-lateral naval logistics coordination agreements for rapid storm relief operations.
  • Renewed legal pact affirming freedom of navigation through international straits and economic exclusive zones.
  • Establishment of the Pacific Atoll Climate Infrastructure Financing Facility.
"Open seas and stable shorelines are indispensable prerequisites for Asian trade and American commercial security alike." — Pacific Maritime Working Group
