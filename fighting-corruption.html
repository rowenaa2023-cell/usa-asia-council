Our Missions · Rule of Law

Countering Global Corruption & Kleptocracy

Strategic corruption is no longer just a domestic governance failure; it is weaponized by authoritarian states to co-opt foreign officials, subvert democratic decision-making, and secure illicit geopolitical leverage. By siphoning public wealth into offshore havens, kleptocrats undermine faith in open markets and hollow out developing state institutions.

Combating transnational kleptocracy demands closing regulatory loopholes in Western financial centers. The United States must rigorously implement the Corporate Transparency Act to unmask anonymous shell companies, hold corrupt enablers (bankers, lawyers, and real estate facilitators) legally accountable, and freeze illicit assets through targeted Global Magnitsky designations.

We advocate for equipping investigative journalists and civil society whistleblowers globally with legal defense resources, empowering multilateral anti-bribery task forces, and linking international development assistance directly to measurable anti-corruption reforms.
