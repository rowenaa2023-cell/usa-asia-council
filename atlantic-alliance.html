Our Missions · Transatlantic Security

The Atlantic Alliance & NATO

For over three-quarters of a century, the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) has served as the indispensable anchor of American and European collective security. Transatlantic unity remains the ultimate guarantor against state aggression, democratic erosion, and coercive intimidation on the European continent.

In an era defined by revanchist autocracies, maintaining unwavering commitment to Article 5 collective defense is essential. Robust defense burden-sharing across European allies, coupled with streamlined interoperability in cyber warfare, advanced air defense, and critical supply resilience, ensures that the alliance deters confrontation while actively safeguarding global sea lanes and democratic norms.

Bipartisan congressional leadership must resist isolationist impulses and guarantee steady investment in modern deterrence, joint interoperability training exercises, and strategic dialogue to secure our shared transatlantic future.
