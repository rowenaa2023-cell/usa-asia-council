Our Missions

China: Strategic Competition

Our approach to China emphasizes strategic competition while maintaining open lines of diplomacy to prevent conflict.

As Beijing expands its military presence in the South China Sea and leverages its Belt and Road Initiative, the U.S. must work closely with regional allies. Our focus is on safeguarding trade routes, protecting intellectual property, and ensuring that the Indo-Pacific remains free and open for all nations.
