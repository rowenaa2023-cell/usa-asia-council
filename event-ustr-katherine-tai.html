Conversation with USTR Ambassador Katherine Tai

Date & Time September 25, 2024
11:00 AM – 1:00 PM EST
Location / Venue National Press Club Ballroom, Washington, DC
Featured Speakers Ambassador Katherine Tai (United States Trade Representative), Trade Advisory Council Members

Executive Overview

United States Trade Representative Ambassador Katherine Tai joined the Council for an exclusive fireside conversation detailing the Biden-Harris Administration’s worker-centered trade policy and its implementation across Asian markets.

Key Deliberations & Strategic Briefing

Ambassador Tai articulated why traditional free trade agreements often overlooked the vulnerabilities of domestic workers and fragile supply chains. She explained how the Indo-Pacific Economic Framework for Prosperity (IPEF) and bilateral trade dialogues with key regional partners are pioneering new standards on labor rights, environmental protection, and supply chain redundancy.

The discussion explored ongoing tariff reviews, critical mineral sourcing agreements, and strategies to prevent predatory non-market trade practices from hollowing out essential domestic industries.

Strategic Policy Takeaways

  • Comprehensive review of IPEF Supply Chain Agreement implementation milestones.
  • Strategies for safeguarding critical technology manufacturing from illicit state subsidies.
  • Enhanced labor monitoring mechanisms embedded in bilateral commercial dialogues.
  • Clarification of U.S. policy regarding targeted strategic tariffs and anti-dumping enforcement.
"A worker-centered trade policy aligns our domestic economic health with our international trade strategy, ensuring that trade creates durable resilience for everyday families." — Amb. Katherine Tai
