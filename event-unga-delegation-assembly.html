GLOBAL DELEGATION

UNGA DIPLOMATIC COUNCIL ASSEMBLY: TRANS-PACIFIC PEACE & SECURITY FORUM

Date & Time September 25, 2026
2:00 PM – 5:30 PM EST
Location / Venue United Nations Delegates Lounge & Plenary Gallery, UNHQ, New York, NY
Featured Participants Council Executive Director Faraz Ullah, Senior Diplomatic Counsellors, UN Regional Representatives

Executive Overview

A joint delegation of Council policy fellows and international diplomats convened at UNHQ for a dedicated working session on trans-Pacific peace, nuclear risk reduction, and regional trade security.

Key Deliberations & Strategic Outcomes

Delegates analyzed progress on multilateral arms control treaties, marine environmental protections in the Pacific, and regional cooperative mechanisms aimed at maintaining stability during geopolitical friction.

Strategic Policy Takeaways

  • Joint communiqué calling for reinforced nuclear confidence-building measures in South Asia.
  • Proposal for an annual Trans-Pacific Maritime Safety and Environmental Protection Working Group.
  • Support for expanding youth and civil society representation in UN disarmament forums.
  • Strengthened ties between non-governmental policy institutes and UN permanent missions.
