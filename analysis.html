Independent Strategic Analysis & Global Geopolitical Commentary

The USA-Asia Council Analysis Series publishes deep analytical essays, geopolitical forecasting, and actionable policy memos authored by Council Senior Fellows, former ambassadors, and intelligence veterans.

Council Senior Fellows and diplomatic experts analyzing Asian geopolitical shifts and strategic deterrence.

Rigorous, objective strategic analysis is the bedrock of effective foreign policy. The USA-Asia Council Analysis desk provides decision-makers in Washington, Asian capitals, and the international business community with unvarnished, timely insights on emerging geopolitical trends, defense posture adjustments, and macroeconomic volatility.

In the 2025–2026 strategic landscape, our research focuses on four core operational axes: First, calibrating credible deterrence in the Pacific while avoiding catastrophic escalation; Second, revitalizing strategic bilateral partnerships across South and Central Asia—including U.S.–Pakistan counter-terrorism, technology corridors, and regional transit corridors; Third, managing economic interdependence with China through targeted de-risking without broad economic rupture; and Fourth, harmonizing AI governance, export controls, and critical minerals security among Indo-Pacific partners.

Our commentaries are regularly cited in congressional testimony, featured across international media, and utilized by executive branch agencies formulating long-term national security strategies.
