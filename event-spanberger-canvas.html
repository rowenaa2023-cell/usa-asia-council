Council Advocacy Forum with Governor Abigail Spanberger

Date & Time April 22, 2025
11:00 AM – 4:00 PM EST
Location / Venue Northern Virginia Regional Field Office, Henrico & Fairfax Counties, VA
Featured Speakers Abigail Spanberger, Grassroots Organizers, National Security Veterans

Executive Overview

Grassroots foreign policy supporters and national security veterans mobilized across Northern and Central Virginia to support Abigail Spanberger’s historic gubernatorial campaign. With her extensive background in national security and bipartisan public service, Spanberger’s campaign resonated deeply with policy practitioners who understand how domestic state strength directly underpins America’s global standing.

Key Deliberations & Strategic Briefing

Volunteers connected with thousands of Virginia voters to discuss how state-level investments in clean energy technology, aerospace supply chains, and public education bolster national competitiveness on the world stage. Participants emphasized that effective foreign policy begins at home through ethical leadership, economic opportunity, and democratic integrity.

The day concluded with an energized community debrief focused on expanding civic mobilization networks across neighboring mid-Atlantic districts.

Strategic Policy Takeaways

  • Over 4,500 voter households contacted across critical Virginia suburban corridors.
  • Mobilization of foreign policy and defense veterans in non-partisan civic voter registration drives.
  • Highlighting the direct link between state-level economic resilience and international competitiveness.
  • Expansion of local NextGen policy chapters in the Washington-Virginia metropolitan area.
"When we invest in strong, principled communities at home, we project enduring moral clarity and strength across the globe." — Community Mobilization Lead
