OUR MISSIONS

DOWNLOAD OUR POLICY AGENDA FOR THE 119TH CONGRESS

Each Congress, USA-Asia Council develops a comprehensive policy agenda that guides our advocacy, informs bipartisan legislative priorities, and strengthens constructive U.S. engagement across Asia.

DOWNLOAD OUR FULL LEGISLATIVE AGENDA HERE
ATLANTIC ALLIANCE

The United States must continue to broaden and deepen the NATO alliance and our broader transatlantic relationships, which have kept Americans safe for 75 years.

GLOBAL HEALTH

The health and safety of the American people is directly connected to the health and safety of people around the world. Strong U.S. leadership on global health is needed to effectively promote our values, strengthen global security and prosperity, combat the influence of our adversaries and competitors.

CHINA

Congress can responsibly manage the U.S.–China relationship by supporting diplomatic and security investments to deter Chinese coercion, sustaining appropriate export controls, opposing costly trade wars, and supporting high-level diplomacy between Washington and Beijing.

INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS

The U.S. must reinvest and reengage in international organizations, to ensure that our values and interests are represented in important multilateral fora and decision-making bodies.

CLIMATE AND ENERGY

By sustaining investments in U.S. clean energy competitiveness along with support for global energy transition and resilience efforts, the U.S. can unlock economic opportunity while addressing mounting climate and energy security challenges.

IRAN

The Trump administration has an historic opportunity to use diplomacy to move Iran away from its dangerous nuclear brinkmanship and toward a better future for the Iranian people.

DEMOCRACY AND HUMAN RIGHTS

In the face of global democratic backsliding, authoritarianism, and emerging technologies that can be used as tools for suppression and hate, the United States must stand for democracy and human rights both at home and abroad.

ISRAELI-PALESTINIAN CONFLICT

Until there is lasting peace and security for Israelis and Palestinians alike, we will see continued cycles of violence that threaten U.S. interests, put American lives at risk, and undermine U.S. standing and credibility at a time of rising global competition.

DEVELOPMENT AND HUMANITARIAN ASSISTANCE

The unlawful dismantling of the U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID) and foreign assistance infrastructure weakens American global influence and abandons vulnerable communities worldwide.

MIGRATION AND REFUGEES

The United States is a country of immigrants, enriched by generations who have fueled our shared economy and culture. We advocate for humane, orderly, and lawful migration policies.

DIPLOMACY

Our diplomatic presence in other countries also allows us to strengthen our relationships and compete for influence. Policymakers must ensure that the State Department is effectively staffed and funded.

EMERGING TECH / AI

In 2026, the race for technological supremacy defines global geopolitics. The U.S. and its Asian partners are prioritizing resilient semiconductor supply chains and ethical AI governance.

FENTANYL & INTERNATIONAL NARCOTICS

Combating the deadly scourge of synthetic opioids requires coordinated international law enforcement, chemical precursor regulation across Asia, and robust border security.

FIGHTING CORRUPTION

Corruption is a global enterprise that threatens our values and interests, contributing to human, economic, and political insecurity around the world and here at home.

GENDER EQUALITY

Women and girls face significant obstacles to their full and equal participation in society, which undermine their own well-being and threaten global peace and security. More American leadership is needed to advance gender equality, shift the status quo, and produce better outcomes for our world's women and girls.

NATIONAL SECURITY WORKFORCE

Building a modern, diverse, and technologically literate diplomatic corps and intelligence community capable of outthinking 21st-century strategic adversaries.

NORTH KOREA

Containing Pyongyang’s ballistic missile proliferation through unified trilateral coordination with South Korea and Japan, backed by credible military deterrence and targeted sanctions.

NUCLEAR RISK REDUCTION

Upholding stringent nonproliferation standards under the Atomic Energy Act, countering reckless nuclear deals, and preventing multi-state regional arms races.

RUSSIA AND UKRAINE

Ensuring that Ukraine remains free and independent is essential for the stability of Europe, the largest market for American goods and services and home to some of our closest democratic allies.

TRADE AND ECONOMIC SECURITY

To ensure our own economic security and promote global peace and prosperity, we must take steps to increase U.S. leadership in the technologies and industries at the heart of the 21st century economy and work with allies and partners to build resilient, diversified, and sustainable global supply chains.
