The Ongoing Conflict in Ukraine

Date & Time July 09, 2024
10:00 AM – 12:30 PM EST
Location / Venue Rayburn House Office Building, Room 2172, Washington, DC
Featured Speakers House Foreign Affairs Committee Leaders, Ukrainian Diplomatic Envoys, Defense Analysts

Executive Overview

Organized during the pivotal Washington NATO Summit week, this congressional briefing provided members of Congress and policy staffers with an unvarnished assessment of the military, economic, and humanitarian dimensions of the ongoing war in Ukraine.

Key Deliberations & Strategic Briefing

Defense analysts and frontline observers reviewed the effectiveness of Western security assistance, air defense interceptor inventories, and the impact of sovereign Russian asset confiscation mechanisms. The panel addressed how Ukraine’s defense is intimately bound to deterrence in the Indo-Pacific, pointing out that unchecked aggression in Europe directly emboldens revisionist actors across Asia.

Participants discussed bipartisan legislative pathways to ensure reliable, multi-year funding streams for Ukrainian sovereignty and humanitarian recovery.

Strategic Policy Takeaways

  • Detailed audit of security assistance execution and frontline logistics transparency.
  • Analysis of European defense industrial ramp-up and long-term production contracts.
  • Strategic deterrence linkages between Ukrainian resilience and East Asian maritime security.
  • Unanimous affirmation of the illegality of forcible territorial conquest under international law.
"Standing with Ukraine is not charity; it is the vital defense of the rules-based order that prevents great-power conflict worldwide." — HFAC Congressional Briefing Co-Chair
