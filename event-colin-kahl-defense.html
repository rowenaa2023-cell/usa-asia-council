Conversation with Under Secretary of Defense Dr. Colin H. Kahl

Date & Time June 18, 2024
3:30 PM – 5:15 PM EST
Location / Venue Pentagon Adjacent Policy Forum, Arlington, VA & Hybrid Webcast
Featured Speakers Dr. Colin H. Kahl (Former Under Secretary of Defense for Policy), Strategic Defense Fellows

Executive Overview

Former Under Secretary of Defense for Policy Dr. Colin H. Kahl joined the Council for a comprehensive analysis of the 2024 National Defense Strategy, integrated deterrence in the Indo-Pacific, and managing simultaneous security challenges in Europe and the Middle East.

Key Deliberations & Strategic Briefing

Dr. Kahl broke down the Pentagon’s concept of "integrated deterrence," which weaves together military capabilities across domains (air, sea, land, space, and cyberspace) with economic statecraft and multilateral alliances. He explored the historic expansion of U.S.-Japan-South Korea trilateral security cooperation and enhanced defense ties with Australia, the Philippines, and regional Asian partners.

The dialogue also addressed emerging defense technologies, including autonomous swarming systems, hypersonics, and quantum sensing, and how democratic nations can lead ethically in their deployment.

Strategic Policy Takeaways

  • Operational definition and practical execution of Integrated Deterrence across the Pacific.
  • Progress report on Camp David trilateral defense agreements with Japan and South Korea.
  • Analysis of defense industrial base bottlenecks and munitions stockpile requirements.
  • Framework for ethical norms and AI command-and-control safety in modern warfare.
"Integrated deterrence means demonstrating to any adversary that the costs of aggression will far outweigh any conceivable benefit. Alliances are our greatest asymmetrical advantage." — Dr. Colin H. Kahl
