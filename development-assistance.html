Our Missions · Foreign Aid

Development & Humanitarian Assistance

U.S. foreign assistance represents less than one percent of the federal budget, yet it yields outsized returns for American security, trade, and moral standing. Investing in global poverty alleviation, famine relief, clean water, and democratic institution-building fosters stable trading partners and prevents state failure.

When America retreats from global development, adversarial powers step in with predatory infrastructure lending and corrupt influence networks. Protecting the statutory mission and operational independence of the U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID) ensures that assistance is delivered efficiently, transparently, and aligned with democratic values.

We advocate for sustained humanitarian disaster relief funding, modernizing digital aid delivery, and strengthening partnerships with frontline local NGOs to save lives and build economic resilience worldwide.
