Our Missions · Arms Control

Nuclear Risk Reduction & Nonproliferation

We are entering an extraordinarily perilous new nuclear era. Decades-old bilateral arms control frameworks between the United States and Russia have collapsed, while China rapidly expands and modernizes its nuclear arsenal. At the same time, regional nuclear flashpoints in South Asia, the Middle East, and the Korean Peninsula multiply the danger of miscalculation.

Reducing nuclear risk requires renewed American diplomatic leadership to reestablish bilateral crisis communications, reinforce hotlines, and pursue trilateral arms control dialogues. The United States must strictly defend the "gold standard" nonproliferation safeguards of Section 123 of the Atomic Energy Act, preventing the reckless transfer of sensitive enrichment or reprocessing technologies to regional states.

We advocate for robust funding of the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA), modernizing American command-and-control systems against cyber intrusions, and establishing international norms prohibiting the integration of autonomous AI into nuclear launch authority.
