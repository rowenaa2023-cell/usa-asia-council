CAPITOL HILL YOUTH INITIATIVE: EMPOWERING YOUNG VOICES IN FOREIGN POLICY

Date & Time March 27, 2026
8:30 AM – 5:30 PM EST
Location / Venue United States Capitol Visitor Center & Congressional Meeting Rooms, Washington, DC
Featured Participants Bipartisan Members of Congress, Council Youth Mentors, Emerging Foreign Policy Scholars, Student Delegates

Executive Overview

The Council hosted its flagship youth empowerment event: "Capitol Hill for All the Youth: Engage, Learn, Make an Impact." Over 150 students, young scholars, and early-career diaspora professionals converged on the U.S. Capitol complex to meet lawmakers, learn policy drafting, and explore careers in international relations.

Key Deliberations & Strategic Outcomes

The day featured interactive advocacy workshops, legislative simulation sessions, and direct meetings with House and Senate foreign affairs staffers. Participants presented youth-authored policy memorandums on climate migration, human rights protections, and educational exchange programs.

Strategic Policy Takeaways

  • Over 40 direct congressional meetings conducted by youth delegations with members of the House and Senate.
  • Launch of the Council's 2026 NextGen Mentorship Program pairing students with former ambassadors and senior analysts.
  • Presentation of the Youth Foreign Policy White Paper to the Congressional Asian Pacific American Caucus.
  • Commitment to expand the Capitol Hill Youth Delegation initiative to a nationwide semi-annual program.
"The future of foreign policy belongs to the next generation. By opening the doors of Capitol Hill to diverse youth voices, we ensure America's diplomatic future remains visionary and inclusive." — USA-ASIA Council Diplomatic Assembly
