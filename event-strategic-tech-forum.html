Upcoming Strategic Dialogue

U.S.–Asia Strategic Technology Forum 2026

Date & Time October 28, 2026
10:00 AM – 4:30 PM PST
Location / Venue Palace of Fine Arts & Hybrid Global Broadcast, San Francisco, CA
Featured Speakers Under Secretary of State for Economic Growth, Chief Science Advisors, Semiconductor Industry Leaders

Executive Overview

The U.S.–Asia Strategic Technology Forum convenes government officials, technological pioneers, and economic strategists to examine the future of artificial intelligence governance, semiconductor manufacturing resilience, subsea data cable defense, and next-generation telecommunications. Asia remains the epicentre of microelectronics fabrication, and securing these pipelines is a paramount national security priority.

Key Deliberations & Strategic Briefing

Key sessions will address the implementation of friend-shoring initiatives, export control harmonizations, and clean-energy data infrastructure across East and Southeast Asia. Participants will dissect how coordinated multilateral investment between American tech consortia and Asian manufacturing hubs can prevent single-point-of-failure vulnerabilities in advanced silicon manufacturing.

Special attention will also be devoted to cyber defense pacts and workforce development programs that train technicians, silicon design engineers, and software architects across both shores of the Pacific.

Strategic Policy Takeaways

  • Launch of the Trans-Pacific Critical Minerals & Semiconductor Reliability Taskforce.
  • Harmonization guidelines for AI safety standards and cross-border commercial data governance.
  • Public-private commitments exceeding $4.2B in resilient clean-tech hardware assembly hubs.
  • Joint technical standards for deep-sea telecommunications cable surveillance and emergency repair protocols.
"The technology alliance between the United States and Asian industrial partners is the bedrock of 21st-century economic security and innovation." — Strategic Tech Forum Chair
