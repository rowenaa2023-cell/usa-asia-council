U.S.–Asia Strategic Voting Record & Legislative Tracker (119th Congress)

The USA-Asia Council Foreign Policy Partnership Scorecard provides an authoritative, non-partisan evaluation of congressional voting patterns, committee actions, and legislative sponsorship affecting U.S. diplomatic engagement, trade resilience, and bilateral cooperation across Asia.

The United States Capitol, Washington, DC — Monitoring 119th Congress roll-call votes on Indo-Pacific trade, Pacific maritime security, and State Department diplomatic appropriations.

The 119th United States Congress faces a pivotal juncture in defining the architecture of American engagement in Asia. As great power competition intensifies and economic supply chains undergo profound realignment, congressional legislation directly influences bilateral trade flows, technology export controls, critical mineral pacts, and regional stability.

The USA-Asia Council Legislative Scorecard tracks every major floor vote and key committee markup in the Senate Foreign Relations Committee, House Foreign Affairs Committee, and Armed Services Committees. Our methodology evaluates lawmakers across three foundational pillars: Diplomatic Investment (funding for diplomatic missions, embassies, and cultural exchanges), Trade Modernization & Supply Chain Security (CHIPS Act oversight, Indo-Pacific digital economy standards), and Principled Conflict Prevention (defense authorizations aimed at deterring maritime conflict while preserving open communication channels).

During the 2025–2026 legislative sessions, over 48 foreign policy-related measures were analyzed, providing constituents, business leaders, and civil society with transparent, data-driven insight into whether their representatives champion constructive international engagement or retreat into counterproductive isolationism.

Strategic Recommendations for the 119th Congress & Executive Leadership

  • Reauthorize Diplomatic Capacity: Fully fund U.S. diplomatic missions across East, South, and Southeast Asia to match economic footprint with skilled Foreign Service personnel.
  • Fast-Track Critical Minerals Bilateral Accords: Enact targeted legislative frameworks guaranteeing tariff relief and technical assistance for allied mineral processing hubs.
  • Mandate Strategic Technology Dialogue: Institutionalize regular congressional reporting on multilateral semiconductor packaging, AI security guardrails, and quantum computing collaborations.
