Climate Geopolitics & Regional Stability

Climate change is no longer just an environmental issue; it is the ultimate geopolitical threat multiplier in Asia. From rising sea levels threatening Southeast Asian coastal cities to shifting monsoon patterns devastating agricultural output in South Asia, environmental crises are reshaping diplomacy.

In 2026, U.S. foreign policy has actively integrated climate resilience into its security alliances. By investing in clean energy infrastructure and exporting green technologies to developing Asian nations, the U.S. is not only countering the monopolization of critical minerals by rival powers but also stabilizing vulnerable economies. Joint initiatives in green hydrogen and disaster-relief coordination demonstrate that sustainable energy policies are the bedrock of future regional stability.
